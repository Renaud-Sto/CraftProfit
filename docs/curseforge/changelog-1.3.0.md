**1.3.0** (WoW Forever 1.60.1)

Added
- **Shopping lists.** "Add to shopping list" in the main window adds the recipe x your number of crafts to your list; adding the same recipe again adds up its crafts. Open the list from the new "Shopping list" button at the auction house, from the options window, or with `/cp shop`. It shows the recipes you planned (with a "x" to take one back) and every material with how many are in your bags, how many are needed and what the missing ones cost (the cheaper of the auction house and the vendor), plus the total left to buy. A material your bags already cover turns grey. Click a material at the auction house to search the missing amount.
- **One list per character, open another's.** Standing at the auction house on an alt? Switch to your main's list with the button at the top of the window and buy for it: "In bags" counts your main's bags as last seen plus yours.
- **Use bags** option (off by default): the materials already in your bags are not counted as bought, so the cost and the result count only what you still have to buy (10 crafts needing 10 herbs with 6 in your bags: you pay for 4). Each material says how many are in your bags. Thanks for the suggestion!
